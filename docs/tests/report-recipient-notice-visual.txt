Recipient confirmation notice visual fix
========================================

Implementation source: b283eaaca37a865d988d1083d17b49ec322f0330

Problem
-------
In a light desktop workspace, ActualRecipientNotice retained the dark theme's
module-initialized card color. The result was a large near-black bar above the
composer, visually dominating the conversation.

Change
------
- Resolve the notice palette at render time from the active theme.
- Use restrained pale green (sent) and amber (queued) semantic surfaces with
  matching outlines in light and dark themes.
- Reduce vertical padding, use a small status dot, and keep recipient identity
  on one compact line.
- Shorten the legacy-Hub fallback without inferring recipient identity.
- Preserve polite status accessibility and the labelled dismiss action.
